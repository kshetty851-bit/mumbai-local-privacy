Privacy Policy

Mumbai Local: Rush Hour (Android, package com.karthikshetty.mumbailocal)
Last updated: 14 November 2026

Summary

The game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It works fully offline.

What the app stores

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, coins, upgrades and settings) is saved on your device, in the app's private storage. The app itself never sends it anywhere. If you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include this small save file in your own Google backup so your progress returns when you set up a new phone; that backup is controlled by you and Google, not by the developer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes the copy on your device.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No internet access: the app does not request the Internet permission.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases. The game is bought once on Google Play.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Google Play

Purchases and downloads are handled by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy. Google may provide the developer with aggregated, anonymous crash and performance statistics (Android vitals). These contain no personal information.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.
